Getting Started

Four steps to your first AutoPilot workflow.

AutoPilot runs as an MCP server connected to Claude. No software to install. No specialists required.

Connect to Claude

In Claude.ai, go to Settings → Integrations → Add Integration. Enter the AutoPilot server URL and authenticate. AutoPilot appears as a connected tool instantly.

Choose your module

Tell Claude what you want to do — automate a workflow, build a dashboard, forecast a trend, model finances, plan growth, or communicate a goal. AutoPilot routes to the right tool.

Describe in plain English

No technical skills required. Describe your process, your data, or your goal in plain language. AutoPilot collects the inputs it needs and passes them to Claude for a complete, structured output.

Execute and iterate

AutoPilot outputs are ready to use — flow JSON to paste into Power Automate, dashboard specs to build in Power BI, financial models to share with your team. Chain tools together for end-to-end automation.
